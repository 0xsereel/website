Built on open rails.

We ship on the standards your counsel already recognizes. No proprietary lock in. No re-papering when you scale.

● Standard 01

CMTA
CMTAT

Capital Markets & Technology Association

The Swiss tokenization framework adopted by major banks and recognized by Singapore's Project Guardian. Sereel is one of the few engineering teams certified to deploy on it.

  • Reference implementation for tokenized securities under Swiss DLT law
  • 40+ institutional members including UBS, SIX, and Sygnum
  • Native compliance modules: transfer restrictions, snapshots, freeze
  • Composable with ERC-3643 for permissioned transferability
● Standard 02

ERC
3643

Permissioned Token Standard / T-REX

The leading EVM standard for compliant security tokens. Identity-on-chain through ONCHAINID, with modular compliance enforced at the contract layer.

  • Built-in eligibility checks: KYC, accreditation, jurisdiction
  • ONCHAINID identity portability across issuers and chains
  • Backed by Tokeny, Apex, ING, and 20+ regulated issuers
  • Sereel deploys CMTAT + ERC-3643 hybrid contracts for cross-jurisdictional issuances

Questions, answered.

A short primer for institutional teams evaluating tokenization infrastructure.

Why CMTA over a proprietary stack?+
CMTA is the institutional tokenization standard adopted by major Swiss banks and recognized by Singapore's Project Guardian. Tokens stay portable, interoperable, and compliant across 40+ institutional members. They are not trapped inside one vendor.
What can we tokenize?+
Securities and equity, bonds and fixed income, private credit, stablecoins, utility tokens, and structured products. Across every jurisdiction listed above. If the asset is real, we can wrap it.
How does custody actually work?+
Enterprise EOAs with multi-signature governance and configurable approval workflows. Shamir Secret Sharing for distributed recovery. Role-based access for treasury operations, and full audit trails for compliance.
How do we get started?+
