● Standard 01

CMTA
CMTAT

Capital Markets & Technology Association

The Swiss tokenization framework adopted by major banks and recognized by Singapore's Project Guardian. Sereel is one of the few engineering teams certified to deploy on it.

  • Reference implementation for tokenized securities under Swiss DLT law
  • 40+ institutional members including UBS, SIX, and Sygnum
  • Native compliance modules: transfer restrictions, snapshots, freeze
  • Composable with ERC-3643 for permissioned transferability
  • Audited, open source, and version-locked at the protocol layer
● Standard 02

ERC
3643

Permissioned Token Standard / T-REX

The leading EVM standard for compliant security tokens. Identity-on-chain through ONCHAINID, with modular compliance enforced at the contract layer.

  • Built-in eligibility checks: KYC, accreditation, jurisdiction
  • ONCHAINID identity portability across issuers and chains
  • Backed by Tokeny, Apex, ING, and 20+ regulated issuers
  • Sereel deploys CMTAT + ERC-3643 hybrid contracts
  • Recovery, partitioning, and forced-transfer hooks built in
